Welcome to cabellite mediaroom Help

Everything you need to know to get up and running. cabellite mediaroom is a browser-based TV interface for live channels — all from M3U playlists you provide.

Getting started
The Home menu
The Home menu is your hub. Across the top are tabs: Live TV, Packages, Search, and Help.
Managing M3U playlists
Loading a live TV playlist
1
Open Home → Packages.
2
Click the Upload Live TV Playlist entry.
3
Select one or more .m3u or .m3u8 files.
Loading another playlist adds to your channels. It does not replace existing ones.
Removing a playlist
On the Packages tab, find the Loaded Playlists tray at the bottom. Click the red × next to a playlist to remove it.
What's inside an M3U file
Each entry in an M3U has metadata followed by a stream URL. cabellite mediaroom reads:
  • tvg-logo — the channel's logo image
  • group-title — the channel's category
  • tvg-chno — an explicit channel number (optional)
  • The URL on the next line — the stream source
Bare URLs (one per line) are also supported.
Watching TV & changing channels
Watching a channel
From Home → Live TV → Channels, click any channel card in the list on the left.
Changing to a channel by number
While on the Channels screen or the Guide, just type a channel number.
  • A box appears in the bottom-right corner.
  • Press Enter to jump.
  • Or stop typing — after about three seconds cabellite mediaroom jumps automatically.
The Guide
Open Home → Live TV → Guide to see a program grid with time slots. Click a channel row or a program bar to see details, then press Watch.
Fullscreen
Click anywhere on the video to go fullscreen. Press Escape to come back.
Searching live channels
Find a channel by name
Open Home → Search and type a channel name. Results filter as you type.
  • Use ↑ / ↓ to highlight
  • Press Enter to watch
  • Or click a result
The on-screen keyboard
Typing without a keyboard
When you click into any text box, an on-screen keyboard pops up.
  • Click keys to type
  • Switch between ABC, 123, and #+=
  • Drag the keyboard by its title bar
  • Close it with the ×
What saves automatically
Your data persists between sessions
cabellite mediaroom stores everything in your browser:
  • Loaded playlists
  • Keyboard position
Clearing browser data will wipe everything
Warning: Clearing browser data for cabellite mediaroom's origin will lose all playlists.
What happens if you go offline
Automatic offline detection
cabellite mediaroom checks your connection every few seconds. If it fails, a black screen appears. When you reconnect, everything comes back automatically.
